TradingAgents

A research framework that coordinates analyst, researcher, and trader roles over market information. Its generated decisions are research artifacts, not investment advice.

Utility: Domain-specific; kind: framework.

Stars: 118k+; Starred in September: 7k+.

Utilities

  • Study multi-agent financial analysis and debate workflows.
  • Generate inspectable reports using configured market-data and model providers.

Who it is for

  • Researchers evaluating agent-based financial-analysis methods.

Requirements

  • Python 3.11+ or the documented Docker deployment.
  • Configure model access and the market-data providers required by the chosen workflow.

Limitations

  • The project explicitly disclaims financial, investment, and trading advice.
  • Backtests and generated decisions depend on data timing, quality, assumptions, and nondeterministic model output.

Cost and privacy notes

  • Data access, models, and repeated analysis/backtests can incur costs.
  • Do not expose account credentials or treat generated decisions as authorization to trade.

Illustrative example (untested)

Illustrative, not tested: analyze a historical ticker/date and inspect whether every cited input was available at that time.
