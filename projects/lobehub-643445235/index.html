LobeHub

A workspace for creating, scheduling, and collaborating with agent teammates. It combines conversational interfaces with operator and integration features.

Utility: Personal agents and workspaces; kind: platform.

Utilities

  • Organize agents and conversations in a shared workspace.
  • Schedule agent tasks and connect supported messaging or tool integrations.

Who it is for

  • Individuals and teams wanting a browser-based agent workspace.

Requirements

  • Use a supported hosted or self-hosted deployment such as Docker.
  • Configure models, persistence, and any messaging integrations.

Limitations

  • The Community License adds commercial derivative-work restrictions.
  • Agent scheduling and collaboration do not eliminate the need for human review.

Cost and privacy notes

  • Hosting, provider usage, and commercial licensing/features may have separate costs.
  • Review conversation storage and connected-model data handling; self-hosting does not imply local inference.
