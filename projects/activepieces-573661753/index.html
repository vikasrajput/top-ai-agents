Activepieces

An automation platform with a visual builder and extensible TypeScript integrations called pieces. Its integrations can also be exposed as MCP tools for agents.

Utility: Workflow and business automation; kind: platform.

Utilities

  • Connect business services in reviewed automation flows.
  • Reuse pieces as tools in an agent or MCP client.

Who it is for

  • Operations teams with developers available to configure integrations and permissions.

Requirements

  • Deploy the documented application or use its hosted service.
  • Configure scoped credentials for each connected system and model.

Limitations

  • Enterprise features are licensed separately from the community code.
  • Connected pieces can change external records; add approvals and test with non-production data.

Cost and privacy notes

  • Hosted plans, enterprise features, and provider/integration usage can have separate charges.
  • Self-hosting still sends data to any external apps and models selected in a flow.
