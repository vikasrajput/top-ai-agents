DeepTutor

A learning workspace connecting tutoring, problem solving, quizzes, research, and reading. Its agents can reuse approved learning context across multiple modes.

Utilities

  • Generate explanations and practice material around a knowledge base.
  • Organize study context through notebooks, memory, and learning workflows.

Who it is for

  • Learners and educators who can review generated teaching material.

Requirements

  • Follow the current Python 3.11+ installation and select required feature modules.
  • Configure model, embedding, parsing, and optional frontend services for the chosen deployment.

Limitations

  • Generated explanations and assessment material can contain errors.
  • Feature modules and connected learning sources have separate compatibility and access requirements.

Cost and privacy notes

  • Models, embeddings, document parsing, media generation, and hosting may be billed separately.
  • Student records and learning histories can be sensitive; review account grants, retention, and external-provider use.

Illustrative, not tested: upload an approved lesson and ask for practice questions, then check their answers against the lesson.
