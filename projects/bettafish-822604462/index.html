BettaFish

A multi-agent system for researching public opinion and social-media material. Its documented workflows combine collection, analysis, and report generation rather than just a chat interface.

Utilities

  • Collect and synthesize material about a public discussion topic.
  • Produce reports using specialized analysis agents and configured data sources.

Who it is for

  • Researchers and analysts who can assess collection legality and source bias.

Requirements

  • Deploy the Python/database stack or documented Docker Compose setup.
  • Configure model and data-provider access appropriate to the selected sources.

Limitations

  • Social-media coverage can be incomplete, biased, or constrained by platform access.
  • Generated sentiment and predictions are not validated outcomes or a substitute for source review.

Cost and privacy notes

  • Model calls, data access, and collection infrastructure can incur separate costs.
  • Respect platform terms and personal-data rules; avoid collecting private or unnecessary individual information.

Illustrative example (untested)

Illustrative, not tested: analyze a public product announcement and compare the report's claims with its original posts.
